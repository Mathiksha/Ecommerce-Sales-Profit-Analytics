E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analytics


Project Overview
This project analyses e-commerce sales, revenue, profitability, and order performance using SQL and Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights by examining revenue trends, profit margins, product performance, customer segments, and regional sales performance.

Project Objectives
* Evaluate overall revenue and profitability.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
* Analyse sales and profit trends over time.
* Compare performance across regions and customer segments.
* Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business performance monitoring.

Tools & Technologies
* MySQL
* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Modelling
* Data Visualization

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Total Revenue: Total revenue generated from orders.
* Total Profit: Total profit generated.
* Total Orders: Number of distinct orders.
* Profit Margin (%): Profit as a percentage of revenue.
* Average Order Value (AOV): Average revenue per order.
* Total Customer
* Revenue YTD

Analysis Performed
* Revenue Analysis: Evaluated revenue performance over time.
* Profitability Analysis: Examined profit and profit margin trends.
* Product Performance Analysis: Compared revenue and profit across product categories and subcategories.
* Regional Analysis: Analysed revenue and profitability across geographical regions.
* Customer Segment Analysis: Compared business performance across customer segments.
* Payment Method Analysis: Explored the distribution of orders by payment method.
* Seasonal Analysis: Investigated revenue and profit patterns across seasons.
* Order Trend Analysis: Examined changes in order volume over time.

Data Preparation & Modelling
* Imported and prepared the dataset for analysis.
* Used SQL to query and validate data.
* Applied Power Query transformations to prepare the data.
* Developed a data model to support analytical reporting.
* Created DAX measures for KPIs and business calculations.
* Designed an interactive dashboard with filters and visualizations.

Key Business Value
* Helps monitor sales and profitability performance.
* Identifies products and regions contributing to business profit.
* Supports data-driven pricing and sales decisions.
* Helps evaluate customer segment performance.
* Provides insights into seasonal trends and business growth opportunities.
